# shunti IME (Android) Privacy Policy

[日本語](privacy-policy-android) | **English**

Last updated: September 2026

- **App name**: shunti IME
- **Package name**: com.shunti.japaneseime.android
- **Developer**: shuntilettuce

---

## Introduction

shunti IME ("the App") is a Japanese input method (IME) for Android provided by the developer shuntilettuce. This policy explains how the App handles data.

---

## No network access

**The App does not have permission to access the internet.** It cannot and does not send what you type, your conversion history, learned data, user dictionary, or clipboard contents to any server or third party. Kana-kanji conversion (AI conversion) runs entirely on your device, using the dictionary and model bundled with the App.

---

## Information handled on the device

| Information | Purpose | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Text being composed | Generating conversion candidates | Not stored |
| Conversion learning (reading–surface pairs you chose, up to 500 readings) | Ordering candidates next time | On the device (the App's private storage) |
| User dictionary (readings, words and parts of speech you registered) | Showing them as candidates | On the device (the App's private storage) |
| Clipboard contents | While the App is the selected keyboard, offering text you copied as a paste chip in the candidate bar | The latest 8 items are kept in memory only, never saved |
| Recently used symbols, settings | Symbol list, vibration and appearance settings | On the device (the App's private storage) |

Data in the App's private storage is deleted when the App is uninstalled. You can erase learning at any time with "学習をリセット" (Reset learning) in the App's settings, and remove user dictionary words one by one there.

---

## Permissions

- **Vibration (VIBRATE)**: used only to vibrate when a key is pressed.

---

## Analytics and advertising

The App uses no analytics, no crash-report uploading, and no advertising.

---

## Children's privacy

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
